# Lumora Systems Product Pricing (2026)

## Glowbeam X2

- Standard edition: 129 euros
- "Studio" edition with a clamp mount and extended arm: 159 euros
- Replacement battery: 34 euros

## Lumora Home Plus Subscription

The free Lumora Home app controls all lamps.
The optional "Home Plus" subscription costs 3.99 euros per month or 39 euros per year.
Home Plus adds circadian lighting schedules, voice assistant integration and usage statistics.

## Discounts

- Students receive 15 % off all hardware with a valid student ID.
- Business customers ordering 20 or more lamps receive a volume discount of 22 %.
- Lumora employees receive one free Glowbeam X2 per year.

## Returns

Customers can return any product within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Return shipping is free within Germany and Austria.

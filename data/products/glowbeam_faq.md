# Glowbeam X2 – Frequently Asked Questions

## What is the Glowbeam X2?

The Glowbeam X2 is a smart desk lamp made by Lumora Systems.
It automatically adjusts color temperature between 2700 K and 6500 K based on the time of day.
The lamp connects to the "Lumora Home" app via Bluetooth Low Energy.

## How long does the battery last?

The Glowbeam X2 has a built-in 4800 mAh battery.
At 50 % brightness the battery lasts about 11 hours. At full brightness it lasts about 6 hours.
A full charge over USB-C takes roughly 2.5 hours.

## How do I reset the lamp?

Hold the touch button on the base for 12 seconds until the light blinks orange three times.
After the reset, the lamp must be paired with the Lumora Home app again.

## What is the warranty?

The Glowbeam X2 comes with a 3-year warranty that covers hardware defects.
The battery is covered for 2 years or 800 charge cycles, whichever comes first.
Water damage is not covered by the warranty.

Lumora Systems Remote Work Guidelines

Lumora Systems follows a hybrid work model. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
Tuesdays are "Anchor Days": every team member is expected to be in the office on Tuesdays for planning meetings and team lunches.

Employees who want to work fully remote for a longer period (for example while travelling) can apply for a "Workation".
A Workation can last at most 4 weeks per year and must be approved by the team lead.
Workations are only possible from countries inside the European Union because of tax and data protection rules.

Home office equipment: every employee receives a one-time home office budget of 650 euros for a desk chair, monitor or other equipment.
Receipts must be submitted through the expense tool "Ledgerly" within 60 days.

Core hours: regardless of location, all employees must be reachable between 10:00 and 15:00 Central European Time.
Outside core hours, employees are free to organize their working time.

# Lumora Systems Vacation Policy

## Annual Vacation Allowance

Every full-time employee at Lumora Systems receives 27 paid vacation days per calendar year.
Part-time employees receive vacation days proportional to their contracted hours.
Employees who have worked at Lumora for more than five years receive 3 additional "loyalty days" per year.

## Requesting Vacation

Vacation must be requested in the internal tool "TimeHarbor" at least 14 days in advance.
Requests for more than 10 consecutive working days require approval from both the team lead and the department head.
During the annual "Quiet Week" in the last week of December, the whole company is closed and no vacation days are deducted.

## Carrying Over Vacation

Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over into the next year.
Carried-over days expire on March 31st of the following year.
Unused days beyond that limit are forfeited and are not paid out.

## Sick Days During Vacation

If an employee becomes sick during vacation and provides a doctor's note, the sick days are not counted as vacation days.
The doctor's note must be uploaded to TimeHarbor within 3 days after returning to work.

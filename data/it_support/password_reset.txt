Password Policy and Password Reset

Lumora account passwords must be at least 14 characters long.
Passwords expire every 180 days. TimeHarbor, Ledgerly and email all use the same Lumora account.
The last 8 passwords cannot be reused.

Resetting a forgotten password:
Go to the self-service page "reset.lumora.internal" and confirm your identity with the LumoraAuth mobile app.
If you have lost your phone, call the IT helpdesk at extension 4040. The helpdesk is staffed Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 18:00.
For security reasons, the helpdesk will never ask for your current password.

Account lockout:
After 6 failed login attempts the account is locked for 20 minutes.
Accounts locked three times within 24 hours must be unlocked manually by the IT helpdesk.
